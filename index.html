This is a screening tool, not a ruling. It reads the ingredient list and flags what needs checking. It cannot know how an animal was slaughtered, what a factory shares equipment with, or what an undisclosed "natural flavouring" is made from. Anything marked Needs checking means ask the manufacturer or look for certification — not that it is forbidden.
